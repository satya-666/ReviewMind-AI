    How ReviewMind AI works

    Choose Analyze a review for local classifier inference, or Ask for shopping advice for provider-backed conversation. The sample button fills an editable smartphone review; it does not submit it.

    The trained TF-IDF classifier predicts overall sentiment. Product aspects use keyword rules. Scores are not reliability percentages.

    Gemini handles conversation only when configured and you opt in. If Gemini fails, a specific error and Retry are shown instead of a simulated answer. When SerpApi is configured and permitted, product tools retrieve Indian shopping listings. Cards show the exact retrieved evidence; missing specifications and availability stay unknown.

    New Chat starts without the previous chat’s context. Chats are saved to your account until you delete them in History or Settings. Gemini receives bounded recent messages and your user-stated preference notes from this chat, under Google’s data-use terms. Feedback is only for actual sentiment predictions.
